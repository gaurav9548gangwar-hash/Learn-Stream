1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, LearnStream may collect the following information:

  • Name: to identify your student account and personalize the learning experience.
  • Email address: for login, account recovery, important updates and support.
  • Mobile or WhatsApp number: if provided, for verification or support.
  • Account credentials: authentication information used to secure account access. We do not ask users to send passwords to support.
  • Course and activity data: courses accessed, lesson progress, quiz and test results, and study-material activity.
  • Live-class information: attendance, participation, or content you submit during classes, where those features are enabled.
  • Payment information: transaction references, purchase status or subscription status when paid features are used. Payment providers may process card or bank details.
  • Device and advertising data: technical diagnostics, device identifiers, ad interactions or usage information if collected by the app or its service providers.

Only information actually collected by the app or its integrated services should be described as collected.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage student accounts.
  • To provide video courses, live classes, PDF notes and study materials.
  • To save learning progress and quiz or test results.
  • To process subscriptions and provide paid access, where available.
  • To display or measure advertisements, if advertising services are enabled.
  • To respond to support requests and account deletion requests.
  • To maintain security, troubleshoot problems and improve the service.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

We may share information with service providers where necessary to operate LearnStream. Depending on the actual app setup, these may include hosting and database providers, authentication services, video delivery services, live-class platforms, payment processors, analytics providers and advertising networks.

We do not sell personal information for money. Third-party services may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Important: The app operator should identify the actual providers integrated into LearnStream and ensure this description matches their data practices.

4. Advertisements

If advertisements are displayed in LearnStream, the advertising provider may collect information such as advertising identifiers, ad interactions, device information or diagnostic data. The information collected depends on the advertising SDK and its configuration.

Where required, applicable consent and advertising controls will be provided.

5. Payments and Subscriptions

Paid courses or subscriptions may be processed through a payment provider or app-store billing service. LearnStream may receive transaction references and purchase or subscription status to activate access and provide support.

Payment processing is also governed by the relevant provider's terms and privacy policy.

6. Data Retention and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Information is retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, administer accounts, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. Some records may be retained when legally required.

7. Your Privacy Choices and Data Deletion

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

To request account deletion, email fitnessaipro@gmail.com from your registered email address. We may ask for reasonable information to verify account ownership.

Account deletion may remove account details and associated learning data, except information that must be retained for legal or legitimate security purposes.

8. Children and Student Privacy

LearnStream provides educational services. Parents or guardians with questions about a student's personal information may contact us using the email address below.

The app operator must ensure that the app's intended age group, consent practices and handling of children's information comply with applicable laws and Google Play requirements.

9. Third-Party Services and Links

The app may use third-party services or link to external websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policies. The actual third-party services used by LearnStream should be identified accurately in this policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its services or applicable requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page will indicate when the policy was last revised.
